Antone Heyward's task manager, tracked here as reference rather than as something we maintain. It keeps tasks and events local and private in the vault, then syncs them to Google Calendar in real time — so the notifications arrive on the devices you actually carry without the tasks themselves leaving your notes. That two-way shape is the interesting part, and the reason it's in this collection: it's the clearest working answer we've found to "local-first, but I still need a reminder at 3pm." Distributed through BRAT rather than Obsidian's Community Plugins directory; the author describes it, cheerfully, as built for his own needs and entirely vibe-coded.
