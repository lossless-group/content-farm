A long-running community plugin by Haouari Mohammed (nhaouari) that pioneered the idea of executing markdown files as parameterized AI prompts inside Obsidian. We vendor it as a study reference — its `templates/` folder, its handlebars-style interpolation, and its model-agnostic provider config all informed the cf/cft codefence DSL and the per-directory profile templates that ship in perplexed.
